I am the Director of the Computational Imaging Group ([<b>CIG</b>](https://cig.ece.wisc.edu)) and the Leon and Elizabeth Janssen Associate Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering ([<b>ECE</b>](https://engineering.wisc.edu/departments/electrical-computer-engineering)) at [<b>UW–Madison</b>](https://www.wisc.edu). I am also a fellow of the Wisconsin Institute for Discovery ([<b>WID</b>](https://wid.wisc.edu)). I hold affiliate appointments at the School of Computer Sciences ([<b>CS</b>](https://www.cs.wisc.edu/)), the Department of Radiology at the School of Medicine and Public Health ([<b>SMPH</b>](https://www.radiology.wisc.edu/)), and the Data Science Institute ([<b>DSI</b>](https://dsi.wisc.edu/)).

I was previously the Donald L. Snyder Associate Professor at [<b>WashU</b>](https://washu.edu/) from 2017 to 2025, a Visiting Professor at [<b>ENS Paris</b>](https://www.ens.psl.eu/en) in 2024, a Visiting Faculty Researcher at [<b>Google Research</b>](https://research.google/) from 2023 to 2024, and a Research Scientist at Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories ([<b>MERL</b>](https://www.merl.com/)) from 2015 to 2017. I obtained my PhD in 2015 from [<b>EPFL</b>](https://epfl.ch/). Earlier, I was an Exchange Student at [<b>CMU</b>](https://www.cmu.edu/) in 2007–2008, a Visiting Student Researcher at [<b>MIT</b>](https://web.mit.edu/) in 2010–2011, and a Visiting Scholar at [<b>Stanford University</b>](https://www.stanford.edu/) in 2013. I was also an intern at [<b>Microsoft</b>](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/) in 2009.
