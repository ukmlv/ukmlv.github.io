## Course Description

This course covers the mathematical and computational foundations of modern
computational imaging, with an emphasis on inverse problems and machine learning.

Topics include forward models, inverse problems, Bayesian estimation,
optimization, deep learning, plug-and-play methods, diffusion models,
deep equilibrium models, and posterior sampling.
